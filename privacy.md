# TapeScope Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 9, 2026**

TapeScope is a read-only browser extension that displays public market data from Vest Markets.

## Data Collection

TapeScope does not collect, store, sell, or transmit personal user data to TapeScope or to any TapeScope-operated server.

TapeScope does not collect:

- Names, email addresses, or other personally identifiable information
- Account credentials or passwords
- Financial or payment information
- Browsing history
- Location information
- Personal communications
- Keystrokes or general browsing activity

## Market Data

TapeScope connects directly from the user's browser to Vest Markets public API and WebSocket endpoints to retrieve market information such as trade prints, prices, historical market data, and chart data.

This market data is used only to provide TapeScope's market-data visualization features.

## Vest Markets Affiliate Link

TapeScope contains an optional affiliate link to Vest Markets using referral code **WPTAPESCOPE**.

If a user voluntarily clicks this link and later creates or uses a Vest Markets account, the developer of TapeScope may receive an affiliate commission from Vest Markets.

Using the affiliate link is optional and is not required to use TapeScope.

TapeScope does not receive or collect the user's Vest Markets account credentials, trading activity, payment information, or other account information through this affiliate link.

## Local Operation

TapeScope operates locally in the user's browser. The extension does not use a TapeScope backend server to relay or store market data.

## Third-Party Services

TapeScope connects to Vest Markets public infrastructure in order to provide its core market-data functionality. Use of Vest Markets is subject to Vest Markets' own terms and privacy practices.

## Changes to This Policy

If TapeScope's data practices change in the future, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly.

## Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, contact the TapeScope developer through the support information provided on the Chrome Web Store listing.
